RESEARCH DOMAIN

Addressing the challenges of traditional software development through an integrated workflow for requirements engineering, system design, and UI prototyping

Problems with Traditional
Software Engineering

01

Fragmented Development Workflow

Requirements engineering, HLD, LLD, and UI prototyping are handled using separate tools and processes, making it difficult to maintain consistency across development phases.

02

Ambiguous and Incomplete Requirements

Manual analysis of stakeholder conversations and documents can lead to misunderstood needs, missing requirements, and inconsistencies in software specifications.

03

Time-Consuming Design and Prototyping

Producing requirements documents, architectural designs, detailed designs, and UI prototypes manually requires significant time and repeated effort.
